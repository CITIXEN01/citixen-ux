Wis. Stat. § 893.80 Statutory Defense Framework

  • Each notice the Village receives is recorded in a SHA-256 hash-chained log: every entry’s hash includes the one before it, so a later edit to any entry is detectable (tamper-evident).
  • The log lets the Village show when a notice arrived and what was done about it.

A record-keeping tool, not legal advice. It does not by itself confer immunity or establish a statutory defense. Village Attorney review is recommended.
