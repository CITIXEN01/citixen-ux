The National Campaign

Crowd Save America™ is a non-partisan, nationwide civic audit. Citixens snap broken public infrastructure — potholes, dark streetlights, blocked ADA ramps — anonymously, and every report is logged block by block into the public Living Ledger™. The goal: a verified, ground-truth record of all 11 million American blocks.
