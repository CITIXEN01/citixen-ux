ACCOUNTABILITY06 / 10

Field Verification, Not Ghost Closures

A ticket cannot close on paperwork alone. Every resolution requires a timestamped, EXIF-verified proof photo from the crew on site — pushed automatically to the public feed.

BEFORE

Dispatcher marks a ticket "Resolved" from a desk. No photo, no timestamp, no way to confirm a crew was ever on site.

WITH CITIXEN UX

Closure requires a proof photo. EXIF timestamp + location are verified server-side, then the resolution posts to the resident's public feed automatically.
