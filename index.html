    What is CITIXEN UX™?

    CITIXEN UX™ is the modern standard for resident-first municipal engagement. Powered by Civic Intelligence (C.I.), it combines real-time resident reporting with direct city department tracking and transparent project budget auditing—creating an uneditable public record where you hold the receipt to ensure your tax dollars actively protect and improve your neighborhood.

    Crowd Source America™

    Every CITIXEN UX™ report feeds into one unified national ledger—turning individual local fixes into a live, block-by-block picture of civic conditions across the country.
